HOW TO PLAY

  1. WALKClick the street, or use the arrow keys or WASD.
  2. TALKClick a person, or press E next to them. Click the bubble to keep reading.
  3. PICK A MISSIONChoose one in the notepad. Only its people will talk to you.
  4. FOLLOW THE ARROWThe red arrow marks your next stop. Yellow markers mark others the mission needs.
  5. DAY AND NIGHTClick the Day/Night button in the corner. A red arrow over it means your mission needs the other time.
  6. BUILDINGSWalk to a door and click ENTER. Click EXIT to leave.
  7. MINI-GAMESEvery finished mission ends with a short mini-game. Replay any of them from your inventory.
  8. THE REAL HISTORYEach finished mission explains what really happened. Click a reward in your inventory to reread it.
